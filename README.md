# 2V0-17.25-VMware-Cloud-Foundation-9.0-Administrator-Exam-Guide
The VMware Cloud Foundation 9.0 Administrator 2V0-17.25 exam is designed for IT professionals who work with VMware Cloud Foundation environments and need to demonstrate practical knowledge of deploying, configuring, managing, and operating VCF-based private cloud infrastructure. For candidates building their preparation plan, [2V0-17.25 exam questions](https://www.certqueen.com/2V0-17.25.html) from CertQueen can be used as an additional practice resource alongside the official exam objectives. Reviewing questions by topic can help learners identify areas that need further study, particularly across VCF deployment, management, operations, networking, storage, and automation.

## 2V0-17.25 Exam Overview

According to the current Broadcom exam information, the exam has:

**Certification:** VMware Certified Professional – VMware Cloud Foundation Administrator
**Questions:** 60
**Duration:** 135 minutes
**Passing Score:** 300
**Languages:** English and Japanese
**Question Formats:** Multiple Choice and Multiple Choice Multiple Selection
**Exam Delivery:** Proctored through Pearson VUE

## Key Knowledge Areas

The 2V0-17.25 blueprint focuses heavily on VMware Cloud Foundation fundamentals and the deployment, configuration, and operation of VCF environments.

## VMware Cloud Foundation Fundamentals

Candidates should understand the private cloud vision and the role of major VCF technologies, including:

- vCenter
- ESX
- vSphere clusters
- vSphere Supervisor
- vSAN
- NSX
- VCF networking components

Storage knowledge is also important. The exam guide covers vSphere storage, vSAN ESA and OSA, storage policies, cluster deployment, resilience, data availability, and space-efficiency concepts.

Networking preparation should include virtual networking fabrics, connectivity, routing, and virtual networking services.

## Deploy and Configure VCF

The deployment section addresses how VCF environments are established and configured. Important areas include VCF deployment components, deployment models, VCF Network Gateway, VCF Networking, Workload Domains, Workload Domain storage, and Supervisor.

Candidates should understand how these components interact rather than studying each product in isolation.

**Manage VCF**

Management topics include:

- Fleet Management
- Identity Management
- Role-based access control
- License management
- Certificate management
- Password management
- Importing an existing vCenter
- VCF lifecycle management

These subjects are particularly relevant to administrators responsible for maintaining a production VCF environment.

**Operations and Monitoring**

The operations section covers VCF Operations, VCF Operations for Logs, and VCF Network Operations.

Candidates should review:

- Metrics and properties
- Dashboards
- Custom views and reports
- Alerting
- Log monitoring
- Health and diagnostics
- Network monitoring
- vSAN storage monitoring
- Policies
- Application monitoring
- Security hardening
- Compliance
- Configuration drift

A practical understanding of monitoring and troubleshooting workflows can make these topics easier to connect during exam preparation.

**Consume and Automate**

VCF Automation is another important area. Preparation should include VCF Automation use cases, components and deployment options, regions, multi-organization tenancy, provider networking, provider content libraries, organizational administration, organizational networks, content management, extensibility, and governance policies.

Supervisor-based services are also included in this area of the blueprint.

## Who Should Consider This Exam?

Broadcom describes the minimally qualified candidate as someone who can install, configure, manage, and perform basic troubleshooting of a VMware Cloud Foundation solution.

The recommended background includes at least one year of IT experience, foundational Kubernetes and enterprise IT knowledge, and at least six months of experience with VCF or its individual components.

## Preparing for 2V0-17.25

A practical preparation approach is to begin with the official VCF 9.0 documentation and exam objectives, then organize study sessions around the major blueprint areas.

Start with VCF architecture and fundamentals before moving into deployment and configuration. Next, review management and lifecycle operations, followed by monitoring, security, and automation.

Practice questions can then be used to check whether you can apply the concepts in realistic administration scenarios. The goal should be to understand why a particular configuration or operational approach is appropriate rather than simply memorizing answers.
